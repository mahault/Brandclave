"Nothing will change" would have been right
Every suggestion carries a prediction of what a sensor will read six minutes later. It is sealed when made and scored when the time comes. "Nothing will change" is the simplest honest guess, so it is the bar to beat.
Audit trail: every decision, sealed
Each decision is fingerprinted (hashed) the moment it is made. Verify recomputes the fingerprint. Tamper changes a record after the fact, and Verify then shows it was altered.
