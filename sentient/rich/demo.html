Each recommendation is hashed (SHA-256) when it is made, and its forecast is scored when due. Verify recomputes the hash; Tamper edits a stored record to show the mismatch is caught. In this prototype the log is append-only in memory and on disk. It is not yet anchored to an outside timestamp service, so a hash alone does not stop someone with full access from rewriting the whole log.
