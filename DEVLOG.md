## 2026-09-08
- **Sentient prototype, simulation first** (`sentient/`, `docs/SENTIENT_PROTOTYPE.md`). Written the evening after the Driftwood call: Carlos Junior declined to be the capital but offered lab runway for "Sentient inside Hipsip" and asked for a technical session with Mahault on the model and the net differentiator. The doc records the call outcome, the deal shape we want (co-branded inside Hipsip, exclusive for a period; we keep the model and other verticals; no work-for-hire), the IP posture (what we share vs keep in the tech session, NDA first, provisional patent, founder IP written down), and the prototype plan.
- The prototype is a pymdp active-inference agent over a simulated space. Hidden factors: occupancy {empty, some, crowded} and arousal {calm, elevated, stressed}. Modalities: radar count, radar motion, sound level, opt-in wearable HR (with `unknown` carrying mass in every state). Actions: do_nothing, dim_lights, lower_sound, cool_air, staff_alert, plus a rule-based `setback` when the room is confidently and persistently empty. Operator intent and guest purpose are not inferred; they select the preference vector (`build_C`). Copilot pattern: `step()` recommends with reason, confidence and a sealed prediction; `commit()` rolls beliefs forward on what was actually applied; `feedback()` raises the confidence margin for dismissed actions.
- Safety mechanisms, each with a test: do-nothing prior over policies plus a confidence margin; lever mask (an action already at its floor is unavailable); two-step empty streak before setback; staff-alert cooldown.
- Four scenarios, two of them Carlos's objections: `restore_pod` (agent dims and lowers sound, arousal 0.71 -> 0.30, 80% less energy than the fixed preset), `bar_9pm` (intent elevated: 60/60 do_nothing, never fights the loud room), `ambiguous_hr` (elevated HR in an already dim, quiet, cool room: 60/60 do_nothing, no lever), `lobby_peak` (7 staff alerts over 60 steps with cooldown, prediction accuracy 0.61 vs persistence 0.53). Append-only JSONL ledger seals each recommendation with a SHA-256 hash, same rule as the platform Signal Ledger. `python -m sentient.run --scenario all` prints the three numbers (prediction accuracy vs persistence, override rate first vs last third, energy vs preset).
- `SensorFeed` protocol with `SimulatedFeed` and a `HomeAssistantFeed` skeleton so a real room drops in with no model change. 10 new tests green; ruff clean on the package.
- **Second pass, realism.** Rewrote `sentient/space.py` so the world is richer than the model: individual occupants (purpose, sensitivities, valence, wearable, dwell), Poisson group arrivals by hour, temperature inertia, variable staff response, radar miss/ghost, stale frames, dB as a power sum with door/HVAC transients, wearable sync gaps, daylight. Three new scenarios where the agent must cope with being wrong: `happy_group` (staff dismiss the hushing 7 times in the first third, margins rise to 0.24/0.30, recommendations stop), `noisy_sensors` (still calms the room, predictions 0.75 vs persistence 0.65), `sensor_failure` (dead dB meter: sound predictions collapse to 0.07 while persistence stays 1.00, ledger flags the modality). Model fixes: a live wearable is evidence of occupancy; stickier transitions; calming actions differ in strength; the gate walks actions in preference order so a dismissed lever gives way to the next; a dismissed setback raises the empty-streak required. Ledger now reports calibration (per-bin reliability and ECE), dismissals per time third, per-modality accuracy and sensor flags. 16 tests green. Predictions remain underconfident in calm rooms (soft A); learning A from real-room data is the next model step.
- **Found a latent bug in the existing pymdp agents** while doing this: pymdp 1.x with the default `categorical_obs=False` expects integer observation indices, but `user_adaptive_pomdp.py` and `pymdp_learner.py` pass one-hot arrays, which pymdp then reads as indices. Their posteriors are therefore wrong. Not fixed here; flagged for a separate pass.
