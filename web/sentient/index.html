Sentient

Spaces that notice before your guests have to tell you.

Sentient reads a lounge, a lobby or a spa through a few small devices hidden in the space. No cameras, no recordings. It works out what is going on, tells your staff what would help, and writes down every prediction so it can be checked against what actually happened.

    For teams that are already good

    What it adds to great hospitality

    Your best people already read a room. Sentient does not replace that. It covers what no team can do on its own.

    01

    Every zone, every minute

    Nobody can watch the sauna door, the quiet terrace and the lounge at once. Sentient does, and points staff to where they are needed.

    02

    Equipment, before guests feel it

    It notices when a space stops responding the way it should, like heaters that run and give no heat, and calls maintenance. No sensor on the unit is needed.

    03

    Ahead of the queue

    From the door count and the pace of the circuit, it sees a crowd coming and suggests the second sauna or desk before the queue forms.

    04

    The guest nobody noticed

    One guest who paired a watch is having a hard time in a busy, happy room. The room looks fine; their watch does not. Someone goes to them.

    05

    The same care on every shift

    New staff, night shifts, a second property. The standard your best people set becomes something the space helps keep.

    06

    A record you can check

    Every suggestion is written down with what it expects to happen, then scored when the time comes. You see the record itself, not a report you have to trust.

    In plain words

    How it works

    1. Sense.

      Off-the-shelf devices: a small radar for presence and movement, an air monitor, a sound unit that keeps only the loudness, a door counter. Heart rate only from guests who choose to pair their own watch.

    2. Understand.

      It keeps a running picture of the space and the people in it: busy or quiet, settled or tense, too warm or too cold, equipment working or not. It also keeps track of how sure it is about each part of that picture.

    3. Suggest, or hold.

      When something would clearly help, it suggests one thing to staff, with the reason. When it is unsure, it does nothing. Staff accept or dismiss, and it learns from both.

    4. Check itself.

      Each suggestion carries a prediction of what will happen next. That prediction is sealed when it is made and scored later, so anyone can see how often it was right.

    Nothing on the guest

    Private by design

    No cameras. No microphones recording speech. No names or faces. A guest's watch is used only if they pair it themselves, and they can stop at any time.

    Switching Sentient off leaves every space on its normal controls. Building systems are read, not rewired.
